## The Comparison Everyone Is Making

Whenever a tech company becomes the most valuable in the world on a wave of transformational technology, someone will compare it to Cisco at the dot-com peak. The comparison has been made about NVIDIA thousands of times since 2023.

Is it fair? Let's quantify it.

## Cisco at Its Peak (March 2000)

At peak market cap of ~$555 billion (March 2000), Cisco's fundamentals:

- **Revenue (FY2000):** ~$18.9 billion
- **Revenue growth (YoY):** +43%
- **Net income:** ~$2.7 billion
- **Net margin:** ~14%
- **Price/Earnings:** ~200x trailing
- **Price/Sales:** ~29x
- **Market cap / GDP share:** ~3.6% of U.S. GDP

**What Cisco actually built:** The physical infrastructure of the internet. Routers, switches, networking equipment. Revenue was real, customers were real, and the technology was transformative. But the stock price assumed a growth trajectory that the laws of market size made impossible to sustain.

**What happened after:** Revenue peaked in 2001 and did not exceed the 2000 level until 2007. The stock fell −86% from peak. By 2012 — 12 years later — it was still down 75% from its peak.

## NVIDIA at Its Recent Peak (~2024–2025)

At peak market cap of ~$3+ trillion, NVIDIA's fundamentals:

- **Revenue (FY2025, ending Jan 2025):** ~$130 billion
- **Revenue growth (YoY):** ~+122%
- **Net income:** ~$73 billion
- **Net margin:** ~55%
- **Price/Earnings:** ~35–50x trailing (varies by period)
- **Price/Sales:** ~25–30x
- **Market cap / GDP share:** ~11% of U.S. GDP

## The Comparison: Where They're Similar

| Metric | Cisco 2000 | NVIDIA 2024–25 |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Market cap share of GDP | ~3.6% | ~11% |
| P/S ratio | ~29x | ~25–30x |
| Revenue concentration | High (internet build-out) | High (AI build-out) |
| Customer concentration | Telecom + enterprise | Hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META) |
| Technology dependency | Clients needed Cisco gear | Clients need NVDA GPUs |

The similarities are real: both companies were the picks-and-shovels supplier for a transformational build-out. Both had highly concentrated customer bases. Both had valuations that assumed continued dominance.

## The Comparison: Where They're Dramatically Different

| Metric | Cisco 2000 | NVIDIA 2024–25 |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Net margin | ~14% | ~55% |
| Revenue growth | +43% | +122% |
| Competitive moats | Hardware + software stack | CUDA + software ecosystem + H100/Blackwell monopoly |
| Gross margin | ~65% | ~78% |
| Return on equity | Moderate | Extraordinary |

NVIDIA's profitability is categorically different from Cisco's in 2000. A 55% net margin is not a hardware company metric — it's a software/platform business metric. This is a critical distinction.

Cisco's hardware margins eroded as competitors commoditized its products (Huawei, Juniper). NVIDIA's GPU monopoly is reinforced by the CUDA software ecosystem — an extremely high-switching-cost moat that took 15 years to build. Recreating CUDA is not a one-year hardware refresh cycle.

## The Real Risk for NVIDIA

The Cisco comparison misses the more relevant risk: **not commoditization of the current product, but the end of the AI infrastructure build-out cycle.**

Hyperscalers are spending $300–500 billion combined on AI capex in 2024–2025. This cannot grow at the same rate indefinitely. When this capex cycle plateaus or declines, NVIDIA's revenue growth will slow dramatically — regardless of whether its product remains technically superior.

The question is not "is NVIDIA a bubble like Cisco" — it's "when does the AI infrastructure build-out cycle peak, and how much of that is already priced in."

## The Conclusion

NVIDIA is not Cisco 1999. Its fundamentals are far stronger, its moat is deeper, and its profitability is in a different category. But its valuation at 11% of U.S. GDP and 25–30x sales still prices in continued dominance at a time when the capex cycle is maturing.

This is a company worth owning — but not at any price. Timing matters, and the capex cycle is the variable to watch.

## 모두가 하는 비교

변혁적 기술의 물결을 타고 기업이 세계에서 가장 가치 있는 회사가 될 때마다, 누군가는 닷컴 고점의 시스코와 비교한다. 2023년 이후 엔비디아에 대해 이 비교가 수천 번 이루어졌다. 공정한 비교인가? 정량화해보자.

## 시스코 고점 (2000년 3월)

시가총액 ~$5,550억 정점에서 시스코의 펀더멘털:

- **매출:** ~$189억
- **매출 성장률:** +43%
- **순이익률:** ~14%
- **PER:** ~200배

인터넷의 물리적 인프라를 구축했다. 매출은 진짜였고, 기술은 변혁적이었다. 하지만 주가는 시장 규모의 법칙이 지속 불가능하게 만드는 성장 궤도를 가정했다. 고점 이후 주가는 −86% 하락했고, 12년 후에도 여전히 고점 대비 75% 아래였다.

## 엔비디아 최근 고점 (~2024~2025년)

- **매출 (FY2025):** ~$1,300억
- **매출 성장률:** ~+122%
- **순이익률:** ~55%
- **PER:** 후행 기준 ~35~50배
- **P/S:** ~25~30배

## 비교: 유사한 점

| 지표 | 시스코 2000 | 엔비디아 2024~25 |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| GDP 대비 시총 비중 | ~3.6% | ~11% |
| P/S 비율 | ~29배 | ~25~30배 |
| 고객 집중도 | 통신 + 기업 | 하이퍼스케일러 |

유사점은 실재한다: 두 회사 모두 변혁적 빌드아웃의 곡괭이-삽 공급자였다.

## 비교: 극적으로 다른 점

| 지표 | 시스코 2000 | 엔비디아 2024~25 |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| 순이익률 | ~14% | ~55% |
| 매출 성장률 | +43% | +122% |
| 경쟁 해자 | 하드웨어 + 소프트웨어 스택 | CUDA + 소프트웨어 생태계 |

엔비디아의 수익성은 시스코와 범주가 다르다. 55% 순이익률은 하드웨어 기업 지표가 아니다 — 소프트웨어/플랫폼 비즈니스 지표다. 시스코의 하드웨어 마진은 경쟁업체들이 상품화하면서 침식됐다. 엔비디아의 GPU 독점은 구축하는 데 15년이 걸린 CUDA 소프트웨어 생태계로 강화된다.

## 결론

엔비디아는 1999년 시스코가 아니다. 펀더멘털이 훨씬 강하고, 해자가 더 깊고, 수익성이 다른 범주에 있다. 하지만 관련 리스크는 더 단순하다: AI 인프라 빌드아웃 사이클의 성숙. 하이퍼스케일러의 AI capex가 정점을 찍거나 감소할 때 엔비디아의 매출 성장은 극적으로 둔화될 것이다.
