## Three Lenses, One Market

No single framework fully explains Bitcoin's price behavior. The halving cycle alone is too mechanical. On-chain data alone is too backward-looking. Macro alone ignores Bitcoin's specific supply dynamics. But when you overlay all three, a more complete picture emerges.

This post is the synthesis — pulling together everything covered in prior articles into one integrated framework.

## Framework 1: The Halving Supply Shock

The most fundamental driver of Bitcoin's four-year cycle is the halving — the built-in reduction in new Bitcoin supply by 50% every ~210,000 blocks (approximately every four years).

**The mechanism:**
- Each halving cuts the daily BTC entering circulation by half
- If demand remains constant, price must adjust upward to compensate miners and maintain market equilibrium
- This supply shock is not linear — the adjustment takes months to propagate as miners adapt, weaker operators exit, and the reduced supply becomes visible on-chain

**The cycle clock (from prior analysis):**
- Months 0–6 post-halving: accumulation phase, low volatility
- Months 6–18: price appreciation begins, retail attention builds
- Months 16–22: historical peak window
- Months 22–36+: bear market correction, repeat

**The critical caveat:** Each cycle produces diminishing percentage returns as Bitcoin's market cap grows. This is mathematically inevitable — a 10x move from a $1 trillion market cap requires more absolute capital than any prior cycle.

## Framework 2: On-Chain Health Indicators

On-chain data provides a real-time view of what Bitcoin holders are actually doing — separated from what they're saying on social media.

**The four key on-chain cycle stages:**

**Stage 1 — Capitulation (bear market floor):**
- MVRV Z-Score < 1 (market trading below aggregate cost basis)
- Puell Multiple < 0.5 (miners under acute revenue stress)
- SOPR < 1.0 persistently (average holder selling at a loss)
- Exchange inflows high (coins moving to exchanges to be sold)

**Stage 2 — Accumulation (early recovery):**
- MVRV rising from < 1 toward 2
- Long-term holders accumulating (LTH supply increasing)
- Exchange outflows increasing (coins leaving exchanges into cold storage)
- Realized losses declining

**Stage 3 — Bull market (expansion):**
- MVRV 2–5
- Short-term holders (< 155 days) increasing as new buyers enter
- Exchange inflows beginning to tick up as profit-taking increases
- SOPR consistently > 1.05

**Stage 4 — Distribution (late cycle):**
- MVRV > 6
- Long-term holders selling to new buyers
- Exchange inflows surging
- SOPR > 1.10 consistently, then sharp drop as market rolls over

## Framework 3: Macro Liquidity

Bitcoin does not exist in a macro vacuum. Since institutional adoption (2020+), Bitcoin increasingly trades as a macro liquidity asset.

**The key macro variables for Bitcoin:**

**Global M2 money supply:** The aggregate global money supply (especially U.S. + China + Eurozone + Japan) has shown strong correlation with Bitcoin price on a 70–90 day lead. When central banks expand money supply, risk assets including BTC benefit with a lag.

**U.S. Dollar Index (DXY):** As discussed in the DXY post, a weakening dollar is historically associated with BTC outperformance. The dollar and Bitcoin are negatively correlated because both are competing claims on global liquidity.

**Real yields:** Negative real yields (inflation > nominal rate) are historically favorable for non-yielding stores of value including BTC and gold. As real yields move from deeply negative to positive, non-yielding assets face headwinds.

## The Convergence Framework: A Decision Matrix

When all three frameworks agree, conviction is highest:

| Halving Phase | On-Chain Stage | Macro | Combined Signal |
|--------------|---------------|-------|----------------|
| Months 6–18 | Stage 2–3 | DXY falling, M2 expanding | Strong bullish |
| Months 6–18 | Stage 3 | DXY rising, real yields rising | Bullish but headwind |
| Months 18–24 | Stage 3–4 | Any | Late-cycle caution |
| Months 0–6 | Stage 1–2 | Any | Accumulation phase |
| Months 24+ | Stage 4 | DXY rising | Bear market probable |

**Current assessment (June 2026):**
- Halving phase: Month ~14, within the historical 6–22 month bull window
- On-chain: MVRV in Stage 2–3 transition, not yet peak territory
- Macro: DXY declining from 2022 peak, macro environment mixed

The frameworks are not fully aligned (macro remains uncertain) but are not contradicting each other. This is consistent with the mid-cycle correction interpretation rather than a cycle ending.

## The Time Axis: The Most Underappreciated Variable

Throughout all three frameworks, time is the consistent thread. The halving cycle is fundamentally a time-based framework. On-chain accumulation takes months, not days. Macro cycles operate on multi-year scales.

The investors who extract the most value from these frameworks are those who think in months and years, not days and weeks. A framework that says "we are likely in month 14 of a 22-month cycle" is useless for predicting next week. It is quite useful for deciding whether to accumulate, hold, or reduce exposure over the next six months.

This is the core discipline: use these tools to set your time horizon and risk posture, not to find the exact bottom or top.

## 세 가지 렌즈, 하나의 시장

단일 프레임워크로는 비트코인의 가격 행동을 완전히 설명할 수 없다. 반감기 사이클만으로는 너무 기계적이다. 온체인 데이터만으로는 너무 후행적이다. 매크로만으로는 비트코인의 특정 공급 역학을 무시한다. 하지만 세 가지를 겹쳐놓으면 더 완전한 그림이 나타난다.

이 포스트는 이전 글들에서 다룬 모든 것을 하나의 통합 프레임워크로 종합한다.

## 프레임워크 1: 반감기 공급 충격

비트코인의 4년 사이클을 이끄는 가장 근본적인 동인은 반감기 — 약 4년마다 새로운 비트코인 공급을 50% 줄이는 내장된 메커니즘이다.

**사이클 시계:**
- 반감기 후 0~6개월: 매집 단계, 낮은 변동성
- 반감기 후 6~18개월: 가격 상승 시작
- 반감기 후 16~22개월: 역사적 고점 창
- 반감기 후 22~36개월+: 약세장 조정

## 프레임워크 2: 온체인 건강 지표

**4단계 온체인 사이클:**
1. **항복 (약세장 바닥):** MVRV < 1, Puell < 0.5, SOPR < 1.0 지속
2. **매집 (초기 회복):** MVRV 1에서 2로 상승, 장기 보유자 축적
3. **불장 (확장):** MVRV 2~5, 신규 매수자 유입
4. **분배 (후기 사이클):** MVRV > 6, 장기 보유자 신규 매수자에게 매도

## 프레임워크 3: 매크로 유동성

**비트코인에 대한 핵심 매크로 변수:**
- **글로벌 M2:** 중앙은행들이 통화 공급을 확장할 때 70~90일 지연을 두고 BTC가 혜택을 받는다
- **DXY:** 달러 약세는 역사적으로 BTC 아웃퍼폼과 연관
- **실질 금리:** 음의 실질 금리는 무수익 가치 저장 수단에 유리

## 수렴 프레임워크: 의사결정 매트릭스

| 반감기 단계 | 온체인 단계 | 매크로 | 통합 신호 |
|-----------|-----------|-------|---------|
| 6~18개월 | 2~3단계 | DXY 하락, M2 확장 | 강한 상승 |
| 6~18개월 | 3단계 | DXY 상승, 실질 금리 상승 | 상승이나 역풍 |

| 0~6개월 | 1~2단계 | 모든 경우 | 매집 단계 |

## 시간축: 가장 과소평가된 변수

세 프레임워크 모두에서 시간이 일관된 실이다. "사이클 22개월 중 14개월째에 있을 가능성이 높다"는 프레임워크는 다음 주를 예측하는 데 쓸모없다. 향후 6개월 동안 매집할지, 보유할지, 비중을 줄일지 결정하는 데는 꽤 유용하다.
