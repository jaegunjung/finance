Two headlines on consecutive days frame the AI infrastructure debate. On Nov 12, the New York Times, Reuters and Investor's Business Daily reported that SpaceX plans to invest more than $74B in AI data center campuses in Texas and Arizona. On Nov 11, Reuters, Bloomberg and the WSJ reported that SoftBank posted a roughly $7B quarterly loss, citing higher US data center costs and financing expenses.

## What Happened

| Date | Company | Development | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 11 | SoftBank | ~$7B quarterly loss on US data center costs and financing | Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ |
| Nov 12 | SpaceX | Plans $74B+ for AI data center campuses in Texas and Arizona | NYT, Reuters, IBD |
| Nov 8 | Alphabet | $15B Malaysia data center investment (reported) | Motley Fool |
| Nov 6 | OpenAI | CFO says investment in data centers continues despite midterm results | CNBC |

## Why It Matters

- **New commitments are still large.** $74B is a multi-year commitment well above our usual materiality threshold, from a company outside the traditional hyperscaler group. It supports the view that the capex wave is broadening, not ending (see our [Amazon post]({{ '/sp500/2026/10/30/amazon-capex-held-free-cash-flow-negative/' | relative_url }}) for the hyperscaler side).
- **Financing is the pressure point.** SoftBank's loss shows that cost overruns and interest costs can hit investors in the data center chain even when demand is strong. Earlier this month ING was also reported to be seeking buyers for $500M of data center loans (CNBC, Nov 6).
- **Divergence inside the AI trade.** Headlines this week described the trade splitting between semiconductors, infrastructure and big tech, and Nebius fell about 10% on a weak outlook with higher capex.

## Investment Read

- Suppliers of power, networking, memory and construction benefit from new commitments regardless of who finances them; the risk sits with leveraged developers and lenders.
- Watch debt issuance, loan syndication and credit spreads on AI-linked borrowers as the early-warning signal.
- Caveat: details of SpaceX's timeline, funding sources and phasing, and the SoftBank loss breakdown, come from headlines; we could not verify them against primary filings. Check the company releases.

## 왜 중요한가

- **신규 약속은 여전히 크다.** $740억은 전통적 하이퍼스케일러 밖의 기업에서 나온 대형 다년 약속으로, capex 물결이 끝나기보다 확산되고 있음을 뒷받침한다 (하이퍼스케일러 쪽은 [아마존 글]({{ '/sp500/2026/10/30/amazon-capex-held-free-cash-flow-negative/' | relative_url }}) 참고).
- **압력 지점은 자금 조달이다.** 소프트뱅크의 손실은 수요가 강해도 비용 초과와 이자 부담이 데이터센터 밸류체인의 투자자를 타격할 수 있음을 보여준다. 이달 초 ING가 $5억 규모 데이터센터 대출 매각처를 찾는다는 보도도 있었다 (CNBC, 11/6).
- **AI 트레이드 내부의 분화.** 이번 주 보도는 반도체·인프라·빅테크가 갈라지고 있다고 전했고, 네비우스는 약한 전망과 capex 증가로 약 10% 하락했다.

## 투자 관점

- 전력·네트워킹·메모리·건설 공급사는 누가 자금을 대든 신규 약속의 수혜를 받는다. 위험은 레버리지가 높은 개발사와 대출 기관에 있다.
- AI 관련 차입자의 채권 발행, 대출 신디케이션, 신용 스프레드가 조기 경보 신호다.
- 유의: 스페이스X의 일정·조달 방식과 소프트뱅크 손실 내역은 헤드라인 기준이며 1차 공시로 확인하지 못했다. 각 사 발표를 확인할 것.
