## The 200-Week Moving Average: Bitcoin's Long-Term Value Floor

The 200-week moving average (200WMA) is simply the average Bitcoin closing price over the past 200 weeks (approximately 3.8 years). It moves slowly, filtering out all the short-term noise, and represents a long-run consensus on Bitcoin's value trajectory.

Since 2013, the 200WMA has served as a powerful long-term support level. The price of the 200WMA has only gone up — it has never declined — reflecting Bitcoin's long-run upward trajectory.

## The Five Times Bitcoin Broke Below the 200WMA

**Case 1: January 2015**
- 200WMA at time: ~$350
- Price at break: ~$310 (−11% below)
- Duration below: ~8 weeks
- Price 6 months later: ~$290 (still below, continued weakness)
- Price 12 months later: ~$430 (+38% from break point)
- Price 24 months later: ~$1,900 (+513%)

**Case 2: March 2020 (COVID crash)**
- 200WMA at time: ~$6,200
- Price at break: ~$5,700 (−8% below)
- Duration below: ~2 weeks
- Price 12 months later: ~$56,000 (+882%)
- Note: extremely brief; exogenous shock, rapid recovery

**Case 3: June 2022**
- 200WMA at time: ~$22,000
- Price at break: ~$20,000 (−9% below)
- Duration below: ~25 weeks (6 months)
- Price at bottom: ~$15,500 (November 2022)
- Price 12 months from break: ~$30,000 (+50%)
- Price 24 months from break: ~$67,000 (+235%)

**Case 4: Minor touches in 2018–2019 (not full sustained break)**
Several brief tests that held or only broke by a few percent before recovering.

**Case 5: Potential 2025–2026 (if triggered)**
Monitoring required. The 200WMA in mid-2026 is approximately $40,000–$45,000.

## What the Patterns Show

**1. Below-200WMA periods are buying opportunities, not exits.**
In every sustained break below (2015, 2022), the price significantly exceeded the break-point within 24 months. The 2022 case delivered +235% from the break point within two years.

**2. The depth of penetration varies but is limited.**
The worst sustained penetration was approximately −30% below the 200WMA in late 2022. Even that extreme depth was not a permanent departure from the upward trend.

**3. Duration below is typically short in halving bull cycles.**
The COVID break was 2 weeks. Even the 2022 bear market, the worst on record for Bitcoin in percentage terms since 2018, only maintained a sustained break for ~6 months before recovering above.

**4. The 200WMA as a scale-in trigger.**
For long-term investors, a Bitcoin price at or below the 200WMA has historically offered the best risk-adjusted entry points. Not because the price can't go lower — it can — but because the 24-month forward return distribution from these levels has been uniformly positive and large.

## The 200WMA vs. the 21-Week EMA

A secondary indicator worth knowing: the 21-week exponential moving average (21WEMA). This faster-moving average acts as mid-cycle support in bull markets.

**The historical pattern:**
- Price above 21WEMA: bull market intact
- Price below 21WEMA but above 200WMA: correction, watch for recovery
- Price below 200WMA: deep bear market territory

The 21WEMA is to bull market corrections what the 200WMA is to generational support: a gauge of trend health at different time scales.

## Where Is the 200WMA Now?

As of mid-2026, the 200WMA is approximately $40,000–$45,000 and continues to rise at a rate of approximately $1,500–$2,000 per month (reflecting the continued upward drift of the long-run average).

Bitcoin pricing at or above the 200WMA in month 14 of the current halving cycle is consistent with a mid-cycle correction, not a final cycle low. Historical patterns suggest the 200WMA should not be challenged unless the cycle fails — which would require either a macro shock or a fundamental breakdown in Bitcoin adoption.

## 200주 이동평균: 비트코인의 장기 가치 바닥

200주 이동평균(200WMA)은 단순히 지난 200주(약 3.8년)의 비트코인 종가 평균이다. 천천히 움직이며 모든 단기 노이즈를 필터링하고, 비트코인 가치 궤도에 대한 장기적인 컨센서스를 나타낸다.

200WMA의 가격은 오직 올라가기만 했다 — 결코 내려간 적이 없다.

## 비트코인이 200WMA 아래로 내려간 다섯 번

**사례 1: 2015년 1월**
- 이탈 시점: ~$310 (−11%)
- 12개월 후: ~$430 (+38%)
- 24개월 후: ~$1,900 (+513%)

**사례 2: 2020년 3월 (코로나 폭락)**
- 이탈 시점: ~$5,700 (−8%)
- 이탈 기간: ~2주 (극히 짧음)
- 12개월 후: ~$56,000 (+882%)

**사례 3: 2022년 6월**
- 이탈 시점: ~$20,000 (−9%)
- 이탈 기간: ~25주 (6개월)
- 24개월 후: ~$67,000 (+235%)

## 패턴이 보여주는 것

1. **200WMA 아래는 매수 기회이지 청산 신호가 아니다.** 모든 지속적 이탈 사례에서 가격은 24개월 내에 이탈 시점을 크게 상회했다.

2. **침투 깊이는 제한적이다.** 최악의 경우 2022년 말에도 200WMA 아래 약 −30%에 불과했다.

3. **반감기 불장 사이클에서 이탈 기간은 일반적으로 짧다.** 2022년 최악의 약세장도 6개월 만에 회복됐다.

4. **200WMA를 분할 매집 트리거로 활용.** 장기 투자자에게 200WMA 이하의 비트코인 가격은 역사적으로 최고의 위험 조정 진입 시점을 제공했다.

## 지금 200WMA는 어디인가

2026년 중반 현재 200WMA는 약 $40,000~$45,000이며, 월 약 $1,500~$2,000 속도로 계속 상승하고 있다. 현재 반감기 사이클 14개월째에 200WMA 위에서 비트코인 가격이 형성되는 것은 최종 사이클 저점이 아닌 중간 조정과 일치한다.
