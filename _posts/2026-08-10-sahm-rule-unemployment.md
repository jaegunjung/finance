## The Cleanest Recession Indicator We Have

Most economic indicators have nuance, lags, revisions, and false signals. The Sahm Rule is notable for having none of these problems to the degree that others do.

Created by Claudia Sahm (former Fed economist), the rule is simple, transparent, and has a perfect track record since 1970.

## The Rule Defined

**The Sahm Rule triggers when:** the 3-month average unemployment rate rises 0.5 percentage points or more above its 12-month low.

Example: If the 12-month low for unemployment was 3.5%, and the current 3-month average is 4.0%, the Sahm indicator reads 0.5 — a recession is signaled.

The genius of the rule is its focus on the *change* in unemployment, not the level. A 4% unemployment rate is very different in a tightening labor market versus a loosening one. The 0.5% rise captures the early acceleration phase of labor market deterioration — the moment when what was a trickle becomes a trend.

## The Historical Track Record

| Recession | Sahm Trigger Date | Recession Official Start | Lead/Lag |
|-----------|------------------|------------------------|----------|
| 1969–1970 | ~Dec 1969 | Dec 1969 | At start |
| 1973–1975 | ~Nov 1973 | Nov 1973 | At start |
| 1980 | ~Jan 1980 | Jan 1980 | At start |
| 1981–1982 | ~Jul 1981 | Jul 1981 | At start |
| 1990–1991 | ~Jul 1990 | Jul 1990 | At start |
| 2001 | ~Mar 2001 | Mar 2001 | At start |
| 2007–2009 | ~Mar 2008 | Dec 2007 | 3 months lag |
| 2020 | ~Apr 2020 | Feb 2020 | 2 months lag (COVID) |
| 2022–? | Not triggered | No recession declared | — |

**Zero false positives since 1970.** Every Sahm Rule trigger has been associated with a recession. The only cases of small lags (2008, 2020) involve either the worst financial crisis since 1929 or an exogenous pandemic shock.

## Why It Works: The Unemployment Spiral

The mechanism is straightforward. Unemployment rises slowly at first, then accelerates:

1. Companies face slowing demand → freeze hiring, eliminate open positions
2. Layoffs begin in the most exposed sectors (manufacturing, retail, finance)
3. Unemployed workers reduce spending → demand falls further
4. More companies face falling demand → more layoffs
5. The spiral is self-reinforcing

The 0.5% threshold captures step 2–3, just as the spiral is beginning to self-reinforce but before it becomes obvious in headline GDP data. NBER officially declares recessions months *after* they begin, based on comprehensive data revisions. The Sahm Rule identifies them in real time.

## What Happens to Markets When It Triggers

**S&P 500 performance following Sahm Rule triggers:**

| Trigger | 3-month S&P return | 6-month return | 12-month return |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| 1969 | −10% | −12% | −20% |
| 1973 | −5% | −25% | −40% |
| 1980 | −10% | +12% | +25% |
| 1981 | −5% | −8% | +14% |
| 1990 | −12% | −5% | +15% |
| 2001 | −15% | −20% | −25% |
| 2008 | −25% | −40% | −45% |
| 2020 | −30% (immediate) | +40% (V recovery) | +50% |

The range is wide — from devastating (2008) to V-shaped recovery (2020). The COVID case was entirely driven by the unprecedented fiscal and monetary response, not the economic cycle.

**The pattern:** in 6 of 8 cases, the 3-month return following the trigger was negative. In 4 of 8 cases, the 12-month return was negative. A Sahm trigger does not guarantee a prolonged bear market, but it significantly raises the probability.

## The 2022–2026 Context

The Sahm Rule has not yet triggered in the current cycle. Unemployment has risen from its 3.4% low (April 2023) but had not reached the 0.5% threshold to trigger the rule as of late 2025.

The threshold was: unemployment 3-month average reaching 3.9% = trigger.

**What to watch:**
- Monthly jobs report (Bureau of Labor Statistics, first Friday of each month)
- 3-month average unemployment rate
- Distance from the 12-month low

If the Sahm Rule triggers, it should immediately shift portfolio risk posture toward defensive — not necessarily sell everything, but reduce cyclical exposure and extend time horizons.

## Claudia Sahm's Own Caveat

The creator of the rule has noted that the 2022–2023 cycle may have structural differences from prior cycles: immigration-driven labor supply increases may cause unemployment to rise without the same level of demand destruction. She has said the rule should be used as a starting point for analysis, not a mechanical trigger.

With that caveat noted, the track record remains the best of any simple recession indicator.

## 우리가 가진 가장 명확한 경기침체 지표

클로디아 샴(전 연준 이코노미스트)이 만든 이 규칙은 단순하고, 투명하고, 1970년 이후 완벽한 추적 기록을 가지고 있다.

## 규칙 정의

**샴 룰 발동 조건:** 3개월 평균 실업률이 12개월 저점보다 0.5%포인트 이상 상승했을 때.

규칙의 천재성은 실업률의 *수준*이 아닌 *변화*에 집중한다는 것이다. 0.5% 상승은 노동시장 악화의 초기 가속 단계를 포착한다.

## 역사적 추적 기록

1970년 이후 **오신호 없음.** 모든 샴 룰 발동은 경기침체와 연관됐다.

## 작동하는 이유: 실업률 나선

1. 기업이 둔화하는 수요 직면 → 채용 동결
2. 가장 노출된 섹터에서 해고 시작
3. 실직자 지출 감소 → 수요 추가 하락
4. 더 많은 기업이 하락하는 수요 직면 → 더 많은 해고

0.5% 임계값은 나선이 자기 강화를 시작하지만 GDP 데이터에서 명확해지기 전의 2~3단계를 포착한다.

## 발동 시 시장 반응

3~6개월 수익률은 대부분의 발동 사례에서 부정적이었다. 2020년은 전례 없는 재정·통화 대응 덕분에 V자 회복의 명확한 예외였다. 샴 룰 발동이 장기 약세장을 보장하지는 않지만 그 확률을 상당히 높인다.

## 샴 룰 창시자의 경고

클로디아 샴 자신은 이 규칙을 기계적 트리거가 아닌 분석의 출발점으로 사용해야 한다고 말했다. 이민 주도 노동 공급 증가로 인해 2022~2023년 사이클의 구조적 차이가 있을 수 있다고 지적했다.
