## Valuation Without History Is Just a Number

A P/E ratio of 30 means nothing in isolation. It means something specific when you know that the same company traded at P/E 15 in 2015 and P/E 45 in 2021. Context is everything.

This post maps the five largest U.S. tech companies against their own historical valuation distributions — not against each other, and not against the S&P 500 average, which obscures company-specific cycles.

## Why P/E Has Limits — And Why We Use It Anyway

P/E ratio has well-known flaws: it's backwards-looking (based on trailing earnings), distorted by one-time items, and meaningless for companies with negative earnings. For mature cash-generating businesses like the ones here, it remains the most intuitive and widely tracked valuation metric.

For high-growth companies like NVIDIA, forward P/E and PEG ratio (P/E divided by growth rate) are more relevant. We'll note this where applicable.

## Apple (AAPL)

| Period | Trailing P/E | Context |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 2015–2016 low | 10–12x | iPhone saturation fears |
| 2019 | 18–20x | Services pivot recognition |
| 2021 peak | 32–35x | COVID multiple expansion |
| 2022 trough | 22–24x | Rate hike repricing |
| 2024–2025 | 28–32x | AI integration premium |

Apple has traded in a wide range but has rarely sustained above 35x trailing earnings. The current premium reflects the market pricing in a future services and AI contribution that is not yet in the earnings base. If AI monetization materializes, the multiple compresses naturally through earnings growth. If it doesn't, multiple contraction is the mechanism.

## Microsoft (MSFT)

| Period | Trailing P/E | Context |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 2016 Nadella early | 20–22x | Cloud transformation underway |
| 2018 | 25–28x | Azure growth acceleration |
| 2021 peak | 38–42x | Cloud + pandemic multiple |
| 2022 trough | 24–26x | Rate normalization |
| 2024–2025 | 32–36x | Copilot / AI integration |

Microsoft's current valuation sits above its historical median (~25x) but below its 2021 peak. The AI monetization story through Azure and Copilot is more quantifiable than Apple's — Microsoft has reported specific AI ARR contributions. The question is whether current growth rates justify current multiples.

## NVIDIA (NVDA)

NVIDIA requires special treatment. Its trailing P/E is almost meaningless given the pace of earnings growth — a 30x P/E on this year's earnings may be a 15x P/E on next year's, which may be a 8x P/E in two years if guidance is met.

The more relevant metric: **PEG ratio** (P/E divided by earnings growth rate). A PEG near 1.0 is historically considered fairly valued; below 1.0 is cheap on a growth-adjusted basis; above 2.0 is expensive.

| Period | Approx PEG | Context |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| 2022 (pre-AI) | 0.5–0.8 | Gaming slowdown, data center modest |
| 2023 | 1.2–1.5 | AI surge, rapid multiple expansion |
| 2024 peak | 1.8–2.2 | Blackwell cycle, peak AI hype |
| 2025–2026 | 1.4–1.8 | Digestion, hyperscaler capex concern |

The key risk: NVIDIA's current valuation assumes continued hypergrowth in AI infrastructure spending. Any reduction in hyperscaler capex guidance would compress the multiple significantly.

## Alphabet (GOOGL)

Alphabet has historically been one of the more "reasonably valued" mega-cap tech stocks, partly because of its advertising revenue concentration (which the market views as more cyclical) and partly because of its Other Bets segment dragging reported earnings.

| Period | Trailing P/E | Context |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 2016–2018 | 25–30x | Stable search dominance |
| 2021 | 28–32x | Advertising boom |
| 2022 | 17–20x | Ad slowdown, multiple compression |
| 2024–2025 | 22–26x | Search + Gemini integration |

Alphabet at 22–26x is near the lower half of its historical range — reflecting the market's uncertainty about whether AI disrupts or enhances its search monopoly.

## Meta (META)

Meta's valuation history is dramatic. Post-2022 "Year of Efficiency," the stock re-rated from deep value to premium:

| Period | Trailing P/E | Context |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| 2022 trough | 9–12x | Metaverse spending, user growth fears |
| 2023 recovery | 20–25x | Efficiency turnaround |
| 2024–2025 | 26–32x | Advertising recovery + AI ads |

Meta's current valuation reflects a full re-rating from distressed to growth. The base effect comparison is now gone — forward expectations are what drive the valuation from here.

## The Portfolio Implication

No single mega-cap stock looks dramatically cheap on a historical basis in 2026. None looks as extended as in 2021. The aggregate suggests the market is pricing in AI monetization benefits that are beginning to materialize in some companies (MSFT, NVDA) and are still speculative in others (AAPL).

Concentration risk in the S&P 500 (top 5 names = ~25%+ of index weight) means this valuation analysis matters for index investors, not just stock pickers.

## 역사 없는 밸류에이션은 그냥 숫자다

PER 30배는 단독으로는 아무 의미가 없다. 같은 기업이 2015년에 PER 15배, 2021년에 PER 45배에 거래됐다는 것을 알 때 비로소 의미가 생긴다.

이 포스트는 미국 5대 기술 기업을 각자의 역사적 밸류에이션 분포와 비교한다.

## 애플 (AAPL)

| 기간 | 후행 PER | 맥락 |
|------|---------|------|
| 2015~2016 저점 | 10~12배 | 아이폰 포화 우려 |
| 2019 | 18~20배 | 서비스 전환 인식 |
| 2021 고점 | 32~35배 | 코로나 멀티플 확장 |
| 2022 저점 | 22~24배 | 금리 인상 재평가 |
| 2024~2025 | 28~32배 | AI 통합 프리미엄 |

## 마이크로소프트 (MSFT)

| 기간 | 후행 PER | 맥락 |
|------|---------|------|
| 2016 나델라 초기 | 20~22배 | 클라우드 전환 |
| 2021 고점 | 38~42배 | 클라우드 + 팬데믹 |
| 2022 저점 | 24~26배 | 금리 정상화 |
| 2024~2025 | 32~36배 | Copilot / AI |

## 엔비디아 (NVDA)

엔비디아는 특별한 처리가 필요하다. 이익 성장 속도를 감안하면 후행 PER은 거의 무의미하다. 더 관련 있는 지표는 **PEG 비율** (PER ÷ 이익 성장률)이다. PEG 1.0 근처가 역사적으로 적정 가치, 1.0 미만은 성장 조정 기준 저렴, 2.0 초과는 비싸다.

| 기간 | 대략적 PEG | 맥락 |
|------|----------|------|
| 2022 (AI 이전) | 0.5~0.8 | 게이밍 둔화 |
| 2023 | 1.2~1.5 | AI 급등 |
| 2024 고점 | 1.8~2.2 | 블랙웰 사이클, AI 과열 |
| 2025~2026 | 1.4~1.8 | 소화, 하이퍼스케일러 capex 우려 |

## 알파벳 (GOOGL)

| 기간 | 후행 PER | 맥락 |
|------|---------|------|
| 2016~2018 | 25~30배 | 안정적 검색 지배 |
| 2022 | 17~20배 | 광고 둔화 |
| 2024~2025 | 22~26배 | 검색 + Gemini |

알파벳은 역사적 범위의 하반부 근처 — AI가 검색 독점을 교란할지 강화할지에 대한 불확실성 반영.

## 메타 (META)

| 기간 | 후행 PER | 맥락 |
|------|---------|------|
| 2022 저점 | 9~12배 | 메타버스 지출, 사용자 성장 우려 |
| 2023 회복 | 20~25배 | 효율성 전환 |
| 2024~2025 | 26~32배 | 광고 회복 + AI 광고 |

## 포트폴리오 시사점

2026년 기준 어떤 대형 기술주도 역사적 기준으로 극적으로 저렴해 보이지 않는다. 2021년처럼 과도하게 확장돼 보이지도 않는다. S&P 500의 집중 리스크 (상위 5개 종목 = 지수 비중 약 25%+)는 이 밸류에이션 분석이 종목 선택자뿐 아니라 인덱스 투자자에게도 중요함을 의미한다.
