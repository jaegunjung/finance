## The Crypto Capital Flow Map

In a Bitcoin bull market, capital flows first into BTC. As BTC stabilizes and the bull market matures, capital rotates into ETH. As ETH rises and gains attention, capital rotates further into altcoins. This sequential rotation has been observed across multiple cycles.

The ETH/BTC ratio is the primary instrument for tracking this rotation.

## Understanding the ETH/BTC Ratio

The ratio is simply the price of ETH divided by the price of BTC. If ETH is $3,000 and BTC is $60,000, the ratio is 0.05.

A rising ETH/BTC ratio means ETH is outperforming BTC — capital is rotating away from Bitcoin toward Ethereum. Historically, this has been a leading indicator that broader altcoin outperformance is beginning.

A falling ETH/BTC ratio means BTC is outperforming — capital is consolidating into Bitcoin. This is typical in early bull markets and in risk-off environments.

## Historical Ranges and Signals

**ETH/BTC Range History:**

| Period | ETH/BTC Range | Context |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| 2017 peak altcoin season | ~0.12–0.15 | Maximum altcoin speculation |
| 2018 bear market | ~0.02–0.04 | Capital consolidated in BTC |
| 2021 DeFi/NFT peak | ~0.08–0.09 | Strong ETH demand |
| 2022 bear market | ~0.05–0.07 | ETH more resilient than 2018 |
| 2023–2024 | ~0.04–0.07 | ETH underperformance vs. 2021 |
| 2025–2026 | ~0.02–0.04 | ETH at multi-year lows vs. BTC |

**The key thresholds:**
- ETH/BTC below 0.04: historically a strong ETH accumulation signal vs. BTC
- ETH/BTC above 0.07–0.08: altcoin season conditions typically present
- ETH/BTC above 0.10: late-cycle altcoin speculation peak territory

## What the Current Low ETH/BTC Ratio Means

ETH/BTC at 2022–2024 lows suggests several things:

**1. Bitcoin dominance is high.** In the current cycle, BTC has led the rally and ETH has lagged. This is atypical — in cycles 2 and 3, ETH was faster to make new ATHs than BTC. The ETF-driven institutional demand for BTC specifically explains some of this.

**2. The ETH value proposition is being questioned.** Layer-2 scaling has been successful but fragmented ETH activity across many chains. There is a genuine debate about whether ETH fee revenue and therefore ETH demand will recover to 2021 levels.

**3. A low ETH/BTC ratio creates the setup for potential ETH outperformance.** If the Bitcoin cycle continues and risk appetite broadens, the rotation into ETH has more room to run from current depressed ratios. A move from 0.03 to 0.06 is a 100% ETH outperformance vs. BTC — even with BTC also rising.

## The Altcoin Season Leading Sequence

Based on historical observation, the rotation sequence is:

1. BTC dominance peaks and begins declining
2. ETH/BTC ratio bottoms and begins rising
3. ETH makes new highs or substantial gains
4. Major altcoins (SOL, ADA, etc.) begin outperforming
5. Speculative small-cap altcoins have their final blow-off

**We are currently watching for steps 1 and 2.** BTC dominance has been elevated and ETH/BTC has been suppressed. A sustained reversal in both would signal the beginning of the rotation sequence.

## The Risk: ETH Is Not the Same Asset It Was in 2021

The ETH bull case assumes that L2 fragmentation is a temporary problem that ETH will benefit from as an asset. The ETH bear case is that L2s have permanently reduced ETH as a fee-capture asset.

This is a genuine fundamental debate, not just a sentiment issue. The ratio analysis works when ETH's fundamental position is intact. If the L2 ecosystem is permanently redirecting value away from ETH, the historical ETH/BTC ratios may not be achievable in future cycles.

Watch actual ETH network revenue (fees burned) for evidence of which scenario is playing out.

## 크립토 자본 흐름 지도

비트코인 불장에서 자본은 먼저 BTC로 흐른다. BTC가 안정되고 불장이 성숙하면서 자본이 ETH로 순환한다. ETH가 오르고 주목받으면서 자본이 알트코인으로 더 순환한다. 이 순차적 순환은 여러 사이클에 걸쳐 관찰됐다.

ETH/BTC 비율은 이 순환을 추적하는 기본 도구다.

## ETH/BTC 비율 이해하기

비율이 오르면 ETH가 BTC를 아웃퍼폼 — 자본이 비트코인에서 이더리움으로 순환 중. 역사적으로 이것은 더 넓은 알트코인 아웃퍼폼이 시작되고 있다는 선행 지표였다.

## 역사적 범위와 신호

| 기간 | ETH/BTC 범위 | 맥락 |
|------|------------|------|
| 2017년 고점 알트코인 시즌 | ~0.12~0.15 | 최대 알트코인 투기 |
| 2018년 약세장 | ~0.02~0.04 | BTC에 자본 집중 |
| 2021년 DeFi/NFT 고점 | ~0.08~0.09 | 강한 ETH 수요 |
| 2022년 약세장 | ~0.05~0.07 | ETH가 2018년보다 탄력적 |
| 2025~2026 | ~0.02~0.04 | ETH가 BTC 대비 다년 저점 |

**핵심 임계값:**
- 0.04 미만: 역사적으로 ETH 매집 신호
- 0.07~0.08 이상: 알트코인 시즌 조건
- 0.10 이상: 후기 사이클 알트코인 투기 고점

## 알트코인 시즌 선행 순서

1. BTC 도미넌스 정점 후 하락 시작
2. ETH/BTC 비율 바닥 후 상승 시작
3. ETH 신고점 또는 상당한 상승
4. 주요 알트코인(SOL, ADA 등) 아웃퍼폼 시작
5. 투기적 소형 알트코인 최종 블로우오프

**현재 1, 2단계를 주시하고 있다.** BTC 도미넌스가 높고 ETH/BTC가 억눌려 있다.

## 리스크: ETH는 2021년과 같은 자산이 아니다

L2 단편화가 ETH 수수료 수입 자산으로서의 가치를 영구적으로 줄이고 있는지 여부가 진짜 펀더멘털 논쟁이다. 역사적 ETH/BTC 비율 분석은 ETH의 펀더멘털 위치가 유지될 때 작동한다. 실제 ETH 네트워크 수익(소각된 수수료)을 모니터링하라.
