The Nov 3 midterms have changed the Clarity Act's outlook. TradingView's live coverage reported Democrats winning the House, and CryptoTicker.io, The Defiant and BigGo Finance reported Democrats taking the Senate majority. Crypto outlets now frame the bill as "on the clock": the Senate cloture vote failed 49-50 on Sept 15, and the 119th Congress ends on Jan 3, 2027. A bill not passed by then dies and has to be reintroduced in the new Congress. (Final seat counts were still being updated at the time of writing.)

## What Changed

| Item | Before the election | After the election (reported) |
|---|---|---|
| Last Senate vote | Cloture failed 49-50 (Sept 15) | Unchanged |
| House | Republican majority | Democrats won (TradingView) |
| Senate | Republican majority | Democrats reported to have won it (CryptoTicker.io, The Defiant) |
| Remaining window | Lame-duck session | Lame-duck session, ends Jan 3, 2027 |
| Analyst view | TD Cowen: a Democratic Congress would sink the bill for about two years | Now the base case |

## Why It Matters

- **The deadline is hard.** Only the lame-duck session is left. Reintroduction in 2027 would restart the process under a Democratic-controlled Congress, with a different balance on the ethics provisions (officials' crypto holdings) and stablecoin yield, which were the sticking points in September.
- **Odds were already low.** Before the vote, CoinMarketCap cited Galaxy warning that odds were near zero after April, and JPMorgan was calling the path to passage narrow this year. The election result removes most of the remaining upside.
- **Regulators fill the gap.** The SEC and CFTC rulemakings we covered on Sept 18 matter more now. Rules are faster than legislation but easier to challenge in court or reverse.

## Investment Read

The market had largely priced in a failed bill since September, so a large new shock is less likely than a slow drift of sentiment. Crypto-sensitive names (exchanges, miners, stablecoin-related stocks) lose a legislative catalyst. Watch whether Republican leaders try to schedule a lame-duck vote, and whether Democrats signal willingness to negotiate in 2027. Treat any lame-duck headline skeptically until a cloture motion is actually filed.

## 왜 중요한가

- **시한이 명확합니다.** 남은 것은 레임덕 회기뿐입니다. 2027년 재발의는 민주당이 주도하는 의회에서 처음부터 다시 시작해야 하며, 9월에 쟁점이던 공직자 코인 보유 윤리 조항과 스테이블코인 이자 문제의 힘의 균형도 달라집니다.
- **확률은 이미 낮았습니다.** 표결 전부터 CoinMarketCap은 Galaxy의 "4월 이후 확률 0에 근접" 경고를, JPMorgan은 올해 통과 경로가 좁다는 평가를 전했습니다. 선거 결과는 남아 있던 기대를 대부분 지웁니다.
- **규제기관이 공백을 메웁니다.** 9월 18일 글에서 다룬 SEC·CFTC 규정 제정의 비중이 더 커집니다. 규정은 법률보다 빠르지만 소송과 정권 교체로 뒤집히기 쉽습니다.

## 투자 관점

시장은 9월부터 법안 무산을 상당 부분 반영해 왔기 때문에, 큰 충격보다는 심리가 서서히 가라앉는 쪽이 더 가능성 높습니다. 거래소·채굴·스테이블코인 관련주는 입법 모멘텀을 하나 잃습니다. 공화당 지도부가 레임덕 표결을 시도하는지, 민주당이 2027년 협상 의지를 보이는지 지켜보세요. 클로처 동의가 실제로 제출되기 전까지 레임덕 관련 헤드라인은 보수적으로 받아들이는 것이 좋습니다.
